Sketchly is a practice canvas for drawing that tells you what's actually working in your sketch — and what to fix next.

Draw on the canvas, tap AI Feedback, and get a score and specific notes on four fundamentals: proportions, shading, line weight, and composition. Every score is calculated by analyzing the actual pixels of your drawing — not a generic tip pulled at random. The feedback changes based on what you drew.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Sketch on the practice canvas — undo, redo, and clear as you go
2. Tap AI Feedback when you're ready for a read on your sketch
3. See your score broken down by proportions, shading, line weight, and composition
4. Get one clear top tip pointing at whichever area needs the most work
5. Save drawings to your gallery and track your practice over time

FEATURES
- On-device AI feedback: proportions, shading, line weight, and composition, each scored and explained
- Fully offline: the feedback engine runs entirely on your device — no upload, no cloud round trip, no account needed
- Nothing to download: the AI feedback works the moment you install the app, no separate model download
- Simple practice canvas with undo, redo, and clear
- Personal gallery of everything you've sketched

WHY OFFLINE
Sketchly's feedback isn't a chatbot describing your drawing in vague terms. It measures real signal in your pixels — tonal variation for shading, stroke consistency for line weight, canvas balance for composition, shape ratios for proportions — and turns those measurements into a score and a specific, actionable note. Because it all runs on-device, your sketches never leave your phone.

Whether you're warming up, working through a drawing habit, or just want an honest second opinion on a sketch, Sketchly gives you something more useful than a blank canvas: a reason to try the next one.
